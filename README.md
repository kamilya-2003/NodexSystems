# NodexSystems

A responsive business website for Nodex Systems, a structured cabling and network infrastructure company that serves data centers, offices, and commercial buildings.

Live site: https://kamilya-2003.github.io/NodexSystems/Nodex1/

About the project

Nodex Systems needed a professional online presence to show potential clients what they do and the quality of their work. I worked with the company to understand their services and goals, then designed and built the site from scratch.

Built: May – June 2025 Role: Designer and developer

Features
Responsive layout: works on phones, tablets, and desktops, with a mobile navigation menu.
Services overview: data center cabling, rack and patch panels, cable management, testing and certification, and security system installation.
Industries section: shows the types of clients the company serves.
Project gallery: photos of real data center and security camera work, with a JavaScript full-screen viewer and next/previous navigation.
SEO basics: page title and meta description written to help the site appear in search results.
Clear calls to action: contact links throughout the page.
